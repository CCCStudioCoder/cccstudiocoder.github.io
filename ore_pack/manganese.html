    • Manganese ore

    Manganese ore can be finded between Y=10 and Y=100. It spawn in veins of +/- 5 ores, and there are on average 30 veins per chunk (more than coal.) It can be mined with a stone pickaxe or better. It drops 1 raw manganese (no drop if mined with another tools).

    Technical values

    • Hardness: 1.5
    • Blast resistance: 10
    • Light level: 0
    • Light opacity: 15
    • Slipperness: 0.6
    • Jump factor: 1
    • Speed factor: 1

    Raw manganese

    Raw manganese is the base ingredient to obtain manganese ingot. It has no other use.

    Manganese ingot

    Manganese ingot is the base ingredient to obtain manganese block, slab, and stairs.
